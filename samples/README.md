# Public solar business data sample

This is a three-row **illustrative sample**, compiled on 2026-11-03 from each company's own public website. It shows the proposed CSV structure for a possible 25-record paid pilot. It is not a client deliverable, a past sale, a complete directory, or a list of homeowner leads.

Each row includes the company's website, headquarters or primary city shown on its site, publicly stated services, one general business contact route, the source page, and the date checked. A contact URL is used where a general email address was not verified on the cited page. No personal contact details or private data are included. Service claims and contact details can change; verify them again before use.

North Carolina is an example geography only. The buyer's target geography, data fields, acceptance criteria, payment method, and permission to deliver are still unconfirmed.
